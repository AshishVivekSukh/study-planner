# Study Planner

A Pomodoro and spaced-revision study planner that installs on a phone from the browser.

- **Today**: the revisions due today, oldest first
- **Timer**: 25-minute Pomodoros with breaks; each one is logged automatically
- **Topics**: add a lecture once and its five revisions are scheduled 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30 days apart
- **Log**: daily sessions, sleep and wake times, target and actual study hours, wasted time
- **Stats**: monthly hours, average per day and efficiency

Your data stays on your own phone. Use Settings > Backup to move it to another phone.

## Install on Android

1. Open the app link in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu (three dots) and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
